# Core Philosophy

Enterprise AI is not primarily a chatbot problem.

Enterprise AI is increasingly:
- workflow infrastructure
- orchestration infrastructure
- retrieval infrastructure
- governance infrastructure
- operational infrastructure

Most enterprise AI failures are operational failures rather than model failures.

Primary failure modes include:
- governance gaps
- fragmented tooling
- weak retrieval systems
- unclear ownership
- poor workflow integration
- lack of evaluation discipline
- missing lifecycle management
