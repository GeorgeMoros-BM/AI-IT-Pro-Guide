Maps of Content (MoCs) provide semantic navigation and curated conceptual pathways.

Folders alone are insufficient once knowledge systems scale.

---
# Role of MoCs

MoCs should:
- connect related concepts
- establish semantic pathways
- prioritize canonical references
- improve discoverability
- support synthesis

---
# Recommended MoCs

- AI-Governance-MOC
- Enterprise-RAG-MOC
- PromptOps-MOC
- Enterprise-AI-Architecture-MOC
- Agentic-Systems-MOC

---
# MoC Design Principles

Preferred:
- curated links
- high signal density
- conceptual organization
- operational usefulness

Avoid:
- giant unsorted link dumps
- redundant navigation
- decorative structures without retrieval value

---
# Strategic Goal

MoCs should evolve into:
- semantic indexes
- executive navigation layers
- AI retrieval anchors
