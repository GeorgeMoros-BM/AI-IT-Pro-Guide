# Information Flow

The intended operating pipeline:

Research
→ Synthesis
→ Framework
→ Playbook
→ Executive Distillation
→ Publication

This pipeline prevents raw information from bypassing synthesis and operationalization.

---
# Content Hierarchy

Not all content has equal authority.

## Highest Authority
- canonical documents
- governance standards
- validated frameworks

## Medium Authority
- reviewed implementation guidance
- operational playbooks
- executive summaries

## Lowest Authority
- research intake
- draft notes
- exploratory thinking
