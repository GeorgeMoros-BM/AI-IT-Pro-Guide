Define how internal knowledge evolves into publishable enterprise AI content.

# Publication Workflow

Research
→ Synthesis
→ Framework
→ Executive Distillation
→ Publication

This prevents:
- raw note dumping
- low-signal publishing
- incoherent public artifacts

# Publication Standards

Publishable assets should:
- be technically coherent
- be executive-readable
- avoid internal assumptions
- remove client-sensitive information
- maintain high signal density
