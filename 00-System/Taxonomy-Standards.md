# Structural Semantics

## Frameworks
Strategic abstractions and decision models.

## Playbooks
Execution methodologies and operational guidance.

## Architectures
Reusable technical system patterns.

## Executive Assets
Board- and CIO-readable summaries.

## Research
Raw intake and source material.

## Synthesis
Distilled interpretation and emerging insight.

## Operational Systems
Governance and orchestration layer.

---
# Folder Philosophy

Folders provide:
- coarse organization
- operational boundaries

Metadata and links provide:
- semantic relationships
- retrieval quality
- navigation pathways

---
# Naming Philosophy

Use:
- stable terminology
- domain precision
- explicit meanings

Avoid:
- vague shorthand
- unnecessary abbreviations
- temporary naming schemes

---
# Strategic Principle

The taxonomy should remain:
- comprehensible
- scalable
- retrieval-friendly
- operationally useful
