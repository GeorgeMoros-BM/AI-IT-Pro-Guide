## Mission

This vault functions as an Enterprise AI Knowledge Operating System designed to support:

- enterprise consulting reuse
- AI governance and architecture
- executive communication
- reusable delivery assets
- publishing and institutional knowledge development
- AI-assisted retrieval and synthesis

The objective is not to accumulate notes. The objective is to create a coherent operational system for enterprise AI strategy and execution.

# Strategic Principles

## 1. Systems Over Notes
The vault prioritizes interconnected systems over isolated note accumulation.

## 2. Reuse Over Reinvention
Frameworks, playbooks, and templates should compound over time.

## 3. Retrieval Over Storage
Information should be easy to discover, navigate, synthesize, and operationalize.

## 4. Governance Over Sprawl
Canonical definitions and lifecycle controls are required as the vault scales.

## 5. Executive Utility Over Technical Theater
Artifacts should remain decision-useful, operationally grounded, and executable.
