## Purpose

This framework provides a structured mechanism for evaluating enterprise AI maturity across governance, operations, technology, workforce enablement, and business execution.

The goal is not merely to measure adoption. The goal is to determine whether AI capabilities are becoming operationally reliable, strategically aligned, and economically scalable.

---

# Maturity Levels

| Level | Name | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Experimental | Isolated pilots, no governance, ad hoc tooling |
| 2 | Opportunistic | Department-level experimentation with limited standards |
| 3 | Operationalized | Governance, approved tooling, repeatable workflows |
| 4 | Integrated | AI embedded into operational systems and workflows |
| 5 | Strategic | AI treated as enterprise capability and competitive differentiator |

---

# Assessment Domains

## Strategy & Leadership
Evaluate:
- executive sponsorship
- investment alignment
- business capability mapping
- roadmap maturity

## Governance & Risk
Evaluate:
- policy maturity
- AI review process
- evaluation discipline
- data handling controls

## Technology & Architecture
Evaluate:
- approved AI platforms
- integration architecture
- observability
- vendor optionality

## Workforce & Enablement
Evaluate:
- AI literacy
- training programs
- change management
- adoption support

## Operations & Delivery
Evaluate:
- AI intake process
- lifecycle management
- monitoring
- operational ownership

---

# Typical Failure Modes

- Shadow AI proliferation
- Tool sprawl
- No evaluation discipline
- Weak executive sponsorship
- Undefined ownership
- Lack of operational monitoring

---

# Recommended Outputs

- Maturity heatmap
- Capability gap analysis
- Governance roadmap
- Executive briefing
- Prioritized improvement plan
