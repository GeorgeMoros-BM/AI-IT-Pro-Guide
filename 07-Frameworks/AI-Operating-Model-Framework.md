# AI Operating Model Framework

> **TL;DR for the Busy Executive:**  
> Enterprise AI succeeds when organizations establish a repeatable operating model that defines ownership, governance, workflows, funding, enablement, and operational accountability.

---

# Purpose

This framework defines how organizations should operationalize AI capabilities across:
- business units
- IT
- architecture
- governance
- operations
- data
- security
- executive leadership

The operating model determines:
- who owns AI
- how AI is delivered
- how standards are enforced
- how AI investments scale
- how operational accountability is maintained

---

# Core Principle

AI is not a single product.

AI is:
- a capability layer
- an operational system
- a governance challenge
- a workflow transformation engine

Therefore:
AI requires an operating model, not isolated tooling.

---

# Operating Model Archetypes

| Model | Characteristics | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Centralized | Single AI team owns delivery | Strong governance | Bottlenecks |
| Federated | Shared standards, distributed delivery | Scalable | Coordination complexity |
| Embedded | AI capability embedded in business units | Domain alignment | Fragmentation risk |
| Hybrid | Central governance + federated execution | Balanced | Requires maturity |

---

# Recommended Enterprise Pattern

Most mature organizations converge toward:

## Hybrid Federated Model

Characteristics:
- centralized governance
- shared architecture standards
- distributed business execution
- reusable AI platform services
- centralized risk oversight

This model balances:
- speed
- governance
- scalability
- operational flexibility

# Common Failure Modes

## Tool-Centric Thinking

Organizations mistake AI tools for AI operating capability.

---

## No Ownership Clarity

No one owns:
- evaluation
- governance
- lifecycle management
- adoption

---

## Fragmented Adoption

Business units independently deploy incompatible AI stacks.

---

## Governance Without Enablement

Governance exists but approved workflows are unusable.

---

## Pilot Graveyards

Organizations run endless pilots without operational integration.
