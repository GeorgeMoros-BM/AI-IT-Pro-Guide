## Objective

Provide a repeatable mechanism for evaluating and sequencing enterprise AI initiatives based on strategic value, operational feasibility, and organizational readiness.

---
# Evaluation Dimensions

| Dimension | Key Question |
|---|---|
| Strategic Alignment | Does this support enterprise priorities? |
| Business Value | What measurable outcome is expected? |
| Feasibility | Is the data and integration landscape viable? |
| Risk | What governance or operational exposure exists? |
| Adoption Readiness | Will users actually adopt it? |
| Scalability | Can the pattern be reused elsewhere? |

---
# Scoring Model

| Score | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | Weak |
| 4-6 | Moderate |
| 7-8 | Strong |
| 9-10 | High strategic value |

---
# Prioritization Matrix

## High Value / Low Complexity
Immediate pilot candidates.

## High Value / High Complexity
Strategic initiatives requiring executive sponsorship.

## Low Value / Low Complexity
Opportunistic automations.

## Low Value / High Complexity
Defer or reject.
