# Purpose

This playbook defines a repeatable process for launching enterprise AI pilots with measurable business outcomes and controlled operational risk.

---

# Phase 1 - Opportunity Selection

Selection criteria:
- measurable business pain
- accessible data
- strong business sponsor
- operational relevance
- low-to-moderate integration complexity

Avoid:
- unclear ownership
- speculative value
- weak adoption potential

---

# Phase 2 - Solution Definition

Define:
- users
- workflow impact
- success metrics
- data sources
- governance controls
- evaluation methodology

---

# Phase 3 - Pilot Execution

Activities:
- configure tooling
- onboard users
- establish baseline metrics
- execute evaluations
- monitor adoption
- capture operational feedback

---

# Phase 4 - Evaluation

Measure:
- productivity improvement
- adoption rate
- accuracy
- operational fit
- user satisfaction
- governance compliance

---

# Phase 5 - Scale or Exit

Decision paths:
- scale enterprise-wide
- expand selectively
- redesign and retry
- retire initiative
