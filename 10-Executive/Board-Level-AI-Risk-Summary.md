## Strategic Risks

- Uncontrolled AI adoption
- Vendor concentration risk
- Competitive displacement
- Data governance failure

---
## Operational Risks

- Hallucination and misinformation
- Inaccurate automation
- Process dependency risk
- Workflow disruption

---
## Security Risks

- Sensitive data leakage
- Prompt injection
- Third-party exposure
- Identity and access gaps

---
## Governance Risks

- Undefined ownership
- Weak evaluation discipline
- Lack of monitoring
- Inconsistent policy enforcement

---

## Recommended Board Questions

- What governance model exists?
- What data is exposed to external models?
- What operational controls are in place?
- What is the AI investment roadmap?
- How is AI effectiveness measured?
