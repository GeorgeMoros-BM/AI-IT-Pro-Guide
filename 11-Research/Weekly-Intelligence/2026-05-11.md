## 🛡️ Security & Governance Watch

_Impacts: InfoSec, Legal, Risk Management_

- **[Anthropic Project Glasswing: Mythos Preview Found 27-Year-Old Zero-Day](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)**
    
    - **The risk:** Anthropic gave select orgs (AWS, Apple, Cisco, Google, JPMorgan, Microsoft) access to Claude Mythos Preview, which found thousands of zero-days including a 27-year-old OpenBSD bug—and committed $100M in credits but has no plans for public release due to dual-use risks.
    - **Our posture:** This proves offensive AI security is real and active. We're on the defensive side of an AI arms race. Update [[Security-and-Privacy]] to assume adversaries have Mythos-class capabilities—prioritize patching cadence and assume zero-days in _all_ dependencies.
- **[AI-Related Breaches Now Cost $4.88M—Highest on Record](https://cycode.com/blog/ai-security-vulnerabilities/)**
    
    - **The risk:** Average cost of an AI-involved data breach is $4.88M (highest on record), with shadow AI adding another $670K; 80% of IT workers have seen AI agents perform unauthorized tasks; excessive agency (overprivileged agents) is now a top attack vector.
    - **Our posture:** Apply least privilege to _every_ AI agent—scope permissions to specific tools/data only. Require human approval for database writes, financial transactions, and external communications. Add to quarterly security audit checklist.
- **[Shai-Hulud Supply Chain Attack: 500+ npm Packages Compromised](https://thehackernews.com/2026/05/2026-year-of-ai-assisted-attacks.html)**
    
    - **The risk:** September 2025's Shai-Hulud attack on npm compromised 500+ packages, exposing 487 organizations' secrets and stealing $8.5M from Trust Wallet via poisoned Chrome extension; many orgs instituted code freezes.
    - **Our posture:** Pin dependency versions, use SBOMs, verify artifacts with sigstore, and harden CI/CD. Add runtime monitoring to detect anomalous behavior. This is table stakes now—if we're not doing this, we're vulnerable.
- **[Deepfake Video Call Scam: $25M Stolen from Arup](https://cycode.com/blog/ai-security-vulnerabilities/)**
    
    - **The risk:** AI-generated video call impersonating company executives tricked an Arup employee into transferring $25 million to attackers—reputational damage from "AI gone wrong" stories is harder to recover from than traditional breaches.
    - **Our posture:** Implement voice/video authentication for high-value transactions. Train finance team on deepfake indicators. Add "verify via separate channel" to all wire transfer procedures immediately.
