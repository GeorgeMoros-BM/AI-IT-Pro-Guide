## Executive Briefing (Strategy & Market)

    - **Why it matters to us:** Treat single-model dependency as a continuity risk — mandate multi-model fallback for any production workflow and add sudden-suspension clauses to frontier-vendor contracts now.

    - **Why it matters to us:** Pin model-plus-harness combinations in procurement rather than model names alone, since rankings reshuffle on benchmark methodology changes.
- **[Meta Launches Business Agent for Real Enterprise Actions](https://futuretools.beehiiv.com/p/meta-wants-in-on-enterprise)**
    - **What happened:** Meta released a Business Agent that books appointments, closes sales, and processes payments across WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram, leveraging 1M+ business users.
    - **Why it matters to us:** Add Meta to messaging-agent vendor evaluations alongside OpenAI and Anthropic, with specific diligence on data-action governance and payment authorization controls.
- **[Box Survey: Heaviest AI Adopters Plan the Most Headcount Growth](https://x.com/levie/status/2065287110744297809)**
    - **What happened:** A Box survey of 1,640 IT leaders across the US, Japan, and Europe found that the companies adopting AI most aggressively are also planning the largest headcount increases. _(Vendor-sponsored data; treat as directional.)_
    - **Why it matters to us:** Use this as a counterweight to headcount-reduction assumptions in workforce planning, but do not cite it as settled — the labor-impact thesis remains contested.

## Engineering Desk (Building & Architecture)

_Impacts: Software SMEs, Data Analysts, Infrastructure_

- **[Supervisory Agent Loops + VISION.md as a Production Pattern](https://x.com/steipete/status/2063697162748260627)**
    - **What it is:** A shift from direct prompting to closed supervisory loops using persistent VISION.md project docs, a meta-agent ("claw") overseeing sub-agents, and built-in verification via tests and errors.
    - **Action item:** Have an engineering lead prototype VISION.md plus meta-agent supervision on one internal project this sprint and document token cost and verification catch-rate versus single-shot prompting.
    - _Related internal docs:_ [[Prompt-Engineering-Playbook]], [[Agent-Architecture-Guidelines]]
- **[The "Loops" Taxonomy: Token → Agent → Verification](https://x.com/sydneyrunkle/status/2065419460811342114)**
    - **What it is:** Practitioners are formalizing agent design into stacked loops — token loop, agent loop (create_agent), and self-verification loop (deepagents) — instrumented in LangChain, with the "Salty Lesson" that you should scale with more agents rather than fixing things yourself.
    - **Action item:** Instrument one internal agent with explicit token/agent/verification loops and measure verification catch-rate; treat the supervisory structure, not the prompt string, as the unit of design.
    - _Related internal docs:_ [[Agent-Architecture-Guidelines]]
- **[Coral: Governed SQL Data Layer for Agents](https://withcoral.com/)**
    - **What it is:** A local-first open-source layer that exposes APIs, databases, and files to agents as scoped, read-only SQL tables with permission controls.
    - **Action item:** Have data/platform teams sandbox Coral for governed agent data access against the existing RAG pipeline and benchmark permission overhead.
    - _Related internal docs:_ [[Data-Governance-Policy]]
- **[Reserve Slow/Expensive Models for Optimization Loops, Not Everyday Coding](https://x.com/theneurondaily/status/2065539639050744299)**
    - **What it is:** Mitchell Hashimoto's Fable takeaway is that slow, expensive frontier models pay off in targeted optimization loops where an agent can churn, test, and improve toward a goal — not in routine coding.
    - **Action item:** Tier model selection by task: route everyday coding to cheaper/faster models and reserve frontier models for compounding optimization loops to control inference spend.
    - _Related internal docs:_ [[Coding-Agent-Guide]]
- **[Harness Choice Is an Independent Performance Variable](https://x.com/kunchenguid/status/2065345999682568593)**
    - **What it is:** A practitioner claim (single-source, contested) that Claude Code is the worst-performing harness versus opencode and cursor CLI when running the identical model.
    - **Action item:** Evaluate harnesses separately from models in internal benchmarks before standardizing on a coding-agent stack; do not assume model quality transfers across harnesses.
    - _Related internal docs:_ [[Coding-Agent-Guide]]
