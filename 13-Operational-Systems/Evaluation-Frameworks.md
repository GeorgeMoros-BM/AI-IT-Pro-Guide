## Definition

Evaluation frameworks define how AI systems are:
- tested
- measured
- validated
- monitored

across reliability, quality, safety, and operational usefulness.

---
# Core Principle

AI systems without evaluation discipline eventually become operational liabilities.

---
# Evaluation Categories

| Category | Focus |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Correctness |
| Reliability | Consistency |
| Safety | Harm reduction |
| Grounding | Evidence quality |
| Latency | Performance |
| Cost | Economic efficiency |
| Operational Fit | Workflow usefulness |

---
# Evaluation Types

## Static Evaluation
Benchmarking against known datasets.

## Human Evaluation
Expert review and qualitative scoring.

## Regression Testing
Detecting performance degradation over time.

## Operational Monitoring
Real-world production observation.

---
# Enterprise Requirements

Production AI systems should include:
- evaluation datasets
- pass/fail thresholds
- regression monitoring
- operational metrics
- governance review

---
# Common Failure Modes

- no evaluation discipline
- benchmark obsession
- no operational monitoring
- weak grounding validation
- ignoring workflow outcomes

---
# Strategic Insight

Evaluation is becoming one of the most important enterprise AI capabilities.
