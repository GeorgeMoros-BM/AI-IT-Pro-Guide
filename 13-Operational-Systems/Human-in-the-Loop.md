## Definition

Human-in-the-loop (HITL) systems require human oversight, validation, or approval before AI-driven outputs become operational actions.

---
# Purpose

HITL exists to:
- reduce operational risk
- improve accountability
- prevent unsafe automation
- maintain governance control

---
# Typical HITL Patterns

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Review Before Action | Human approves AI-generated email |
| Decision Support | AI recommends, human decides |
| Escalation Workflow | AI flags edge cases |
| Exception Handling | Human handles uncertainty |

---
# Governance Importance

As automation increases:
human oversight becomes more important, not less.

---
# Appropriate Use Cases

HITL is especially important for:
- financial workflows
- legal review
- HR processes
- customer communications
- autonomous execution systems

---
# Common Failure Modes

- humans blindly approving outputs
- unclear accountability
- excessive approval bottlenecks
- lack of operational observability

---
# Strategic Insight

The goal is not permanent human dependence.

The goal is:
controlled autonomy with graduated trust.
