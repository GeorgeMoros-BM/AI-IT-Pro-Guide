# 15-Case-Studies

## Purpose

This folder contains enterprise AI implementation case studies, operational examples, transformation narratives, and applied architecture reviews.

The objective is to capture:
- practical lessons
- implementation realities
- governance outcomes
- organizational patterns
- operational failures and successes

---

# Typical Case Study Areas

- enterprise AI rollouts
- governance implementation
- RAG deployments
- agentic workflow systems
- PromptOps adoption
- AI operating model evolution
- AI platform rationalization
- AI security incidents
- Shadow AI management
- AI-enabled workflow transformation

---

# Design Principle

The goal is not vendor promotion.

The goal is operational understanding.
