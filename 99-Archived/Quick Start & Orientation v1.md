## Golden Rules for Enterprise AI

1. **Never use consumer chat interfaces for company data** - Always use API/developer tiers
2. **Assume outputs are probabilistic** - Build in review processes
3. **Start with RAG, not fine-tuning** - 90% of use cases don't need fine-tuning
4. **Track everything** - You'll need audit logs eventually
5. **Test for bias and hallucinations** - Before your users find them
